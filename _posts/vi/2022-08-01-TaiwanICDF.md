<p>The article was updated on August 1, 2022.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.icdf.org.tw/wSite/np?ctNode=31561">ICDF Scholarship</a> is supported by the Taiwan International Cooperation and Development Fund (IDF). Coincidentally, ICDF originated from an agricultural support project from Taiwan to Vietnam in 1959. Currently, the fund has become an important part in maintaining relations - promoting Taiwan's image to allied countries and regions. Including investment in human resources support, economic development, combating climate change and poverty. Part of the fund's money is used to provide annual scholarships for ICDF's own programs.</p>

<p>Speaking of study programs and scholarships, if you are familiar with Erasmus Mundus, you will realize that ICDF has an organization that is extremely similar to E+. ICDF offers scholarships for certain undergraduate, master's and doctoral programs in Taiwan, with a special focus on economic development, environment, agriculture and public health (<a href="https://www.icdf.org.tw/wSite/DownloadFile?type=attach&file=f1646496795738.pdf&realname=Study+Programs+for+2022+Scholarship+Application.pdf">2022 Study Programs</a>). These programs are all Master by coursework, meaning that you only study and write a final thesis and do not focus on scientific research, especially for master of arts students. You still need to find a supervisor early and do research, so it does not affect anything, especially for master of science students. The scholarship provides NT$12,000 living expenses for undergraduate students, NT$15,000 for master's students and NT$17,000 for doctoral students. You are also exempted from tuition, airfare, books and dormitory fees. Personally, I think this is enough for undergraduate and master's degrees.</p>

<p>In addition, studying at ICDF is also very independent. Because your money comes from the fund, not from your instructors and the school, you are very free. You do not need to participate in any teacher projects, do not have to meet deadlines or be late for projects that affect your graduation. You will graduate automatically =)) Most of your classmates are also international students (Asia, Latin America and island countries), helping you experience a multicultural environment. As for me, there are 5 students, each from a different country.</p><

<p>The application for ICDF includes:</p>
<p>- Passport scan<br>- Transcript, graduation certificate (in English).<br>- Foreign language certificate (IELTS/TOEFL/TOEIC/Direct interview.<br>- 2 recommendation letters.<br>- ICDF scholarship application form.<br>- Other documents (study plan, research proposal, CV, etc.)</p>
<p>You can see other requirements here: <a href="https://www.icdf.org.tw/wSite/ct?xItem=12506&ctNode=31563&mp=2#aC">Eligibility</a></p>
---
<p>The review process consists of 3 steps Main: <a href="https://www.icdf.org.tw/wSite/DownloadFile?type=attach&file=1122812215166.PDF&realname=1122812215166.PDF">Chart of Application Procedure</a><br> - Step 1: Candidates submit their application to the school; then register on the ICDF system to receive an application form; Print this form and send it with other documents to the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Hanoi/Ho Chi Minh City. <br>- Step 2: The embassy and the school select suitable candidates and nominate them to the fund independently.<br>- Step 3: The school and the ICDF fund will meet and decide on the final list of candidates.<br> Therefore, students whose names are on the school's list for admission in June may not necessarily receive support from the fund.</p>

<p>Normally, the ICDF scholarship will open annually from January to mid-March. If you are planning to try your hand at ICDF, you should spend 4 to 6 months before the closing date to prepare your documents, the sooner the better. You can read my experience in preparing documents in my other posts and those of my seniors. In my personal opinion, you should spend at least 2 to 3 months to complete your study plan - this is the most important requirement! If necessary, you should also spend 1-2 months to build a research proposal - especially for those studying for a master's degree in science.</p>

<p>Thông thường, học bổng ICDF sẽ mở hàng năm từ tháng 1 tới giữa tháng 3. Nếu bạn có dự định thử sức với ICDF, các bạn cần dành một khoảng thời gian từ 4 tới 6 tháng trước ngày đóng hồ sơ để chuẩn bị giấy tờ, càng sớm càng tốt. Kinh nghiệm chuẩn bị hồ sơ các bạn có thể xem ở các bài khác của mình và các anh chị đi trước. Theo cá nhân của mình, các bạn cần dành ít nhất 2 tới 3 tháng để hoàn thiện bài viết kế hoạch học tập - đây là yêu cầu quan trọng nhất! Nếu cần thiết các bạn cũng cần dành một khoảng thời gian 1-2 tháng để xây dựng research proposal - đặc biệt là các bạn học thạc sĩ khoa học.</p>
